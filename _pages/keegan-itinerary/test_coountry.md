  <section class="itinerary-story__section itinerary-story__section--opening">
    <div class="itinerary-story__section-label">
      <span>01</span>
      <p>The visit</p>
    </div>
    <div class="itinerary-story__opening-copy">
      <p class="itinerary-story__lead">
        Prosthetic care is not a single moment of invention. It is a chain of
        choices connecting product teams, clinicians, technicians, and the
        people who use the finished device.
      </p>
      <p>
        This visit followed that chain across a regional manufacturer, a
        rehabilitation clinic, and conversations with prosthetic users. The
        goal was to record how priorities shift at each stage—and where they
        remain remarkably consistent.
      </p>
    </div>
  </section>

  <aside class="itinerary-story__detail">
    <p class="itinerary-story__eyebrow">A detail worth noticing</p>
    <h3>The appointment is only part of the process.</h3>
    <p>
      Transportation, time away from work, access to follow-up care, and the
      availability of replacement parts can influence an outcome as much as
      the component selected in the clinic.
    </p>

      <h2>Patterns across the system</h2>

  </section>

  <div class="itinerary-story__findings">
    <article>
      <span>01</span>
      <h3>Fit is a moving target.</h3>
      <p>Bodies, activities, environments, and priorities change—sometimes faster than devices do.</p>
    </article>
    <article>
      <span>02</span>
      <h3>Service shapes technology.</h3>
      <p>A sophisticated component offers limited value when maintenance or follow-up is inaccessible.</p>
    </article>
    <article>
      <span>03</span>
      <h3>Success is personal.</h3>
      <p>Walking symmetry may matter clinically, while comfort, reliability, or discretion matters most personally.</p>
    </article>
  </div>
